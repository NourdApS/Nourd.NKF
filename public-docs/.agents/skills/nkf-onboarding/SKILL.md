# NKF Onboarding

Read and follow the complete public
`integrations/onboarding/nkf-onboarding-protocol.md` procedure.

Work only in the emitted candidate workspace until final onboarding. Resolve
every document representation, preserve authority uncertainty, seal exact
candidate digests, and let the deterministic onboarder generate native YAML
and validate the complete staged project.

Stop on an `NKF-014` deferral diagnostic. Onboarding creates a Draft candidate;
it cannot accept meaning or confirm a Realization.
