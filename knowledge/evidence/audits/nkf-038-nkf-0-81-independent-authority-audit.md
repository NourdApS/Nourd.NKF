# NKF-038 NKF 0.81 Independent Authority Audit

This records the independent audit that
[NKF-038](../../tasks/items/NKF-038-release-nkf-0-81-for-public-adoption-and-reconcile-the-record.md)
requires before the NKF 0.81 authority set is accepted, under the direction
adopted by
[ADR 0138](../../decisions/0138-adopt-the-nkf-0-81-public-adoption-direction.md)
and
[ADR 0139](../../decisions/0139-adopt-the-delta-closure-propagation-repair.md).
Each round was performed by a separate Claude agent instance with no context of
the derivation, working read-only against the branch and verifying every digest
with its own tooling. It is Evidence; it accepts nothing.

## Derivation Under Audit

The candidate set was derived from the accepted NKF 0.8 set by one recorded
mechanical script: a copy of the Specification, companion, evaluation policy,
and release set; a version-label pass that makes 0.81 current and 0.8 the
predecessor while leaving dated history untouched; authored insertions for the
recommended-release catalog contract, the volatile-metadata registry, the
checker's closure recompute obligation, and the window slide; the version
delta seeded by the deterministic registry diff; digests bound by a per-version
binding script; and eight Schemas generated by a per-version generator. Every
round re-ran the generator into a scratch copy and compared bytes.

## Round One

Thirteen findings against commit `f334d33`: six material, seven minor.

| Finding | Class | Repair |
| --- | --- | --- |

| `release-set.yaml` was a stale copy with seven schemas and 0.8 fixtures | material | Deferred to implementation: the release set is derived at cut time and is not part of the accepted authority set, as [ADR 0134](../../decisions/0134-accept-the-nkf-0-8-authority-set.md) established |
| The new recommended-release Schema had no binding rule: absent from the manifest's `schemas` list, present in the enforcement paths without an id, and never stated to be outside project schemas | material | Manifest `schemas` lists eight entries in both Specification and companion; the Schema is stated to validate the catalog only and to be excluded from project schemas; the enforcement paths list exactly the six project schemas |
| The derivation dropped the companion's `governing_decisions` list and its three validation-cannot-accept statements | material | Restored, with [ADR 0134](../../decisions/0134-accept-the-nkf-0-8-authority-set.md) through [ADR 0139](../../decisions/0139-adopt-the-delta-closure-propagation-repair.md) appended |

## Round Three

Against commit `294b578` and the working tree that followed: every round-two
finding repaired with evidence; digest chain closed; strict YAML clean; Schemas
byte-identical on regeneration; no material finding. Two minor findings, both
repaired before acceptance: two prose lines still over eighty columns, and the
companion's `freshness.policy.digest` still carried the NKF 0.6 policy digest
`a870dc03...e4a4`, inherited unchanged through 0.71 and 0.8 and accepted with
them. The 0.81 companion binds the 0.81 policy bytes there.

## Boundary

Three audit rounds establish that the candidate set states the adopted
direction consistently, binds its digests correctly, and carries no
copy-forward from its predecessor the auditors could find. They do not accept
it; the acceptance Decision does that, bound to the digests above. They do not
prove the implementation, which does not yet exist, and they do not confirm
any release candidate.
